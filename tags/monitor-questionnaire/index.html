Monitor Recommendation Questionnaire

Looking for a new monitor? Fill out the questionnaire below so we can help you find one that suits your setup and needs.

Answer all questions in a single message using Shift + Enter for line breaks.

1. System Specifications

What are your current PC specifications?

Include your CPU, GPU and RAM.

2. Budget & Availability

How much are you willing to spend? Include your local currency, country and any preferred retailers.

3. Intended Usage

What will you primarily use the monitor for? Include specific games or applications if applicable.

E.g. competitive gaming, casual gaming, content creation, productivity or media consumption.

4. Resolution

Which display resolution are you aiming for?

E.g. 1080p, 1440p, 4K or Ultrawide.

5. Refresh Rate

What refresh rate are you looking for?

E.g. 60Hz, 144Hz, 240Hz or 360Hz.

6. Size & Curvature

Do you have a preferred screen size, aspect ratio or curvature?

E.g. 24", 27", 32", flat or curved.

7. Panel Technology

Do you have a preference for a particular panel type?

E.g. IPS, VA, TN, OLED or no preference.

8. Monitor Configuration

Are you purchasing a single monitor or multiple monitors? Will you be keeping any existing monitors?

9. Additional Requirements

Are there any specific features or requirements you would like us to consider?

E.g. HDR, VRR, colour accuracy, USB-C, VESA mounting or adjustable stands.

The more information you provide, the more accurate our recommendations will be.
