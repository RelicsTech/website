How to Update Your BIOS

Prepare for a motherboard BIOS update: identify the exact model, save your BitLocker recovery key and follow manufacturer instructions.

How to Update Your BIOS

1. Identify your exact motherboard model and hardware revision, or your exact prebuilt/laptop model. Use the manufacturer’s support page and manual for that model.

2. Back up important files and save your BitLocker recovery key somewhere you can access without this PC. If BitLocker is enabled, follow the manufacturer’s instructions to suspend protection before the update and resume it afterwards.

3. Read the BIOS release notes and update instructions, including any required intermediate version. Download the correct firmware directly from the manufacturer.

4. Prepare and extract the update exactly as instructed. The USB format, file name and update method vary by model.

5. Enter the firmware update utility (often Q-Flash, EZ Flash, M-Flash or Instant Flash) and select the correct file. Use stable power and do not interrupt the update.

6. After completion, review BIOS settings and boot order, then confirm that BitLocker protection has resumed.

Do not turn off the PC while the firmware update is running.
