Good questions

A few things
you might be wondering.

Here's what to know before you join.

Is Relics tech support really free?

Yes. Our tech support and PC building assistance are completely free. Join the Discord server to request help from our community and Support Specialists.

Will I be talking to a person or an AI?

A real person. Relics provides 100% human tech support. Our Support Specialists work with you to understand and troubleshoot your issue.

What computer problems can I ask about?

Ask about PC crashes, hardware and software issues, troubleshooting, and general IT questions. Share your device details, any error messages, and what you've already tried so the team has a useful starting point.

Can you help me build or upgrade a gaming PC?

Yes. Head to PC Building in our Discord server for personalized parts lists and upgrade advice. Tell us your budget, where you're buying, what you'll use your PC for, and any preferences.

Do I need experience with computers to join?

No. Beginners and experienced tech enthusiasts are welcome. You can join for help, learn from others, share your knowledge, or just enjoy the community.

How do I apply to be a Specialist or Staff Member?

Join the Discord server and visit the hiring channel for application information. We're recruiting people who enjoy helping with technology or contributing to the community.
