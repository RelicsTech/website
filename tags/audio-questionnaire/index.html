Audio Recommendation Questionnaire

Looking for a new audio device? Fill out the questionnaire below so we can help you find one that suits your listening preferences and needs.

Answer all questions in a single message using Shift + Enter for line breaks.

1. Budget & Availability

How much are you willing to spend? Include your local currency, country and any preferred retailers.

2. Device Type

What type of audio device are you looking for?

E.g. headphones, gaming headsets, IEMs or earbuds.

3. Intended Usage

What will you primarily use the device for?

E.g. competitive gaming, casual gaming, music, office work or commuting.

4. Open-Back vs. Closed-Back

Do you prefer an open-back or closed-back design?

Open-back headphones generally offer a more spacious soundstage but leak sound and provide little isolation. Closed-back headphones typically offer better isolation and privacy.

5. Connectivity

Do you prefer wired, wireless or both?

6. Sound Preferences

Do you have a preferred sound signature?

E.g. bass-heavy, neutral, warm, bright or no preference.

7. Audio Equipment

What device or audio equipment will you connect your headphones to?

E.g. motherboard audio, dedicated sound card, DAC/AMP, console or smartphone.

8. Previous Audio Experience

Which audio devices have you used before? What did you like or dislike about their sound quality, comfort or functionality?

9. Additional Requirements

Are there any specific features or requirements you would like us to consider?

E.g. microphone, active noise cancellation, battery life, comfort, weight or replaceable cables.

The more information you provide, the more accurate our recommendations will be.
