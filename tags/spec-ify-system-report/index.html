Share a Spec-ify System Report

Prepare a hardware and software report for PC troubleshooting, review privacy settings and share the report with your Discord specialist.

Copy the full text, paste it into your Relics Discord help conversation and add your answers where relevant. Links to Discord channels require server membership. The copy includes Markdown and any reference image link.

Discord tag

Spec-ify

Spec-ify generates a detailed overview of your PC's hardware and software. This information can be very useful when we're trying to identify and troubleshoot issues with your system.

Step 1 - Download Spec-ify

Download Spec-ify from the official website: https://spec-ify.com/

Step 2 - Open Spec-ify

Run Specify.exe after downloading it. Only run the download if you trust its source and have verified that it came from the official website. Ask a specialist if a security warning appears.

If you'd like to keep certain information private, open Settings and disable the collection of details such as your Windows username, serial numbers, and network information.

Step 3 - Run the scan

Click Start and allow Spec-ify to scan your system. Wait until the process has finished.

Step 4 - Share your results

After the scan, Spec-ify will open a webpage containing your system report and automatically copy the URL.

Return to Discord and send the link here.

Review the report before sharing. Do not post passwords, recovery keys or other private information.
