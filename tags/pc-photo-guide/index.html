PC Troubleshooting Photo Guide

Help Discord specialists troubleshoot your PC with clear photos of the motherboard, power connections, graphics card and cables.

Copy the full text, paste it into your Relics Discord help conversation and add your answers where relevant. Links to Discord channels require server membership. The copy includes Markdown and any reference image link.

Discord tag

Provide the Following Pictures

Make sure pictures are NOT BLURRY and at the CORRECT ANGLE.

If you have a modular PSU, also photograph the cables plugged into it.

Photograph the full interior, motherboard model, RAM, graphics card, motherboard power connectors and the rear cable connections. Keep the PC off and unplugged while inspecting internal cables. Never open the PSU enclosure. Avoid showing serial numbers or personal information.

The original example image is currently unavailable; ask your Discord specialist if you need help choosing the angles.
