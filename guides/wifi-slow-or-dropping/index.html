Wi-Fi Slow or Dropping? How to Fix It

Find out first whether the PC or the network is the problem, then fix the likely cause step by step.

  1. Test another way. Connect the PC with a network cable, or test another device on the same Wi-Fi. This shows whether the problem is the PC or the network.
  2. Restart router and PC. Unplug the router for 30 seconds, plug it back in and restart the PC.
  3. Use 5 GHz and get closer. Connect to the 5 GHz network if you have one. It is faster, but walls weaken it, so move closer or move the router.
  4. Update the network driver. Download the Wi-Fi driver from the maker of your motherboard or adapter and install it.
  5. Turn off adapter power saving. Open Device Manager, then Network adapters, open the Wi-Fi adapter, go to Power Management and untick "Allow the computer to turn off this device".
  6. Change the router channel. In the router settings, pick a less crowded channel, or set it to auto. On 2.4 GHz, channels 1, 6 and 11 work best.
  7. Reset the network. As a last step, use Settings, Network and internet, Advanced network settings, Network reset, then restart.

If it still drops, a PC build with a cable or a better Wi-Fi card helps. Ask us in the Discord.
