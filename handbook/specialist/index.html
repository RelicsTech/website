Orientation

Getting Started

The teams, the roles, how to join, and where everything lives.

The three teams

Tech Support

Troubleshooting software and hardware problems. Role: Tech Team.

PC Building

Part lists, upgrades and compatibility. Role: Build / Upgrade Team.

Laptop / Prebuilt

Laptop and prebuilt recommendations. Role: Laptop / Prebuilt Team.

Roles

Junior Specialist, Specialist, Senior Specialist. Promotions come from constant activity, solid work and strong tech skills. The details are on the Policies page.

How to apply

Fill out the Google form. You can apply to one, two or all three teams. After that, follow #specialist-app-results and #specialist-intros.

  • Application review: a reviewer accepts or declines.
  • Team vote: the team gives opinions, the reviewer decides.
  • You confirm your answers are your own work (AI for research only).
  • The role is not a real-world certification.

Operations

Tickets

From the Create Ticket button to the end message: how a ticket runs.

Life of a ticket

  1. Claim it. Click Claim Ticket or use /ticket claim. Read the form answers at the top.
  2. Diagnose. Start simple: restart, drivers, Task Manager. Use tags for repeatable instructions.
  3. Can't continue? Click Unclaim, post in #specialist-chat and leave context for the next person.
  4. Wrap up. Send !endmessage, then /ticket requestclose or /ticket close.

Who types where

Users press Create Ticket in #tech-support or the PC building and support channels. Three kinds exist: support, PC building and application.

  • Tech Team types only in Tech Support tickets.
  • Build / Upgrade Team types only in PC Building tickets.
  • Someone else needs in? Use /ticket add and pick the user or role.

Closing

  • Close after 2 days without a reply, or after 1 day if the opener says to close it.
  • Keep the ticket list free of clutter.
  • Closed tickets and transcripts are in #ticket-logs.
  • If something goes wrong or the user is unhappy, tell a Senior or Management. Don't argue in the ticket.

Adding a note

Notes are saved per user, so the next specialist sees the history. Add one when a user has a repeat issue or there is something the next person should know.

  1. Go to the notes channel. Non-moderators can only use the Notes system in the channel set up for it.
  2. Add the note. Use /note add, pick the user and write the note. You can also use the Vör Discord integration (right-click menu).
  3. Check it. /notes @user lists a user's notes, /note info shows one by its ID.
  4. Fix a mistake. /note edit or /note delete on your own note.
Who may add notes

Junior Specialist, Specialist, Senior Specialist and Tech Team. You can add and read notes, but you cannot see notes written by moderators.

Troubleshooting

Start simple, work toward complex, and reach for a tag when the answer repeats.

Workflow

Check the form answers at the top of the ticket (OS, issue type, description). Ask what changed recently, then go from simple to complex: restart, drivers, Task Manager, then logs and hardware.

  • Check #specialist-resources first.
  • AI is for research only. Verify it, never copy-paste.
  • Basics first.

Which tag for which problem

PC won't POST, odd BIOS state!cmosReset CMOS after checking power and RAM seating.

Right-click a pasted FRST log and pick Clean FRST Log (or use /frst clean_strings) to strip noise before you read it.

Bots

What each bot does and what you need from it.

Heads up

The bots moved recently. If a command does nothing, check it still works and tell a Senior or Management.

Vör active
Tags such as !endmessage, !bhq, !usb. Any specialist can create their own tags. Also reminders, PSU and PCPP tools and notes. Reputation (/solved, !rep, !leaderboard) and Tiers are currently disabled.
Tickety active
Opens and manages tickets through the Create Ticket button. Three kinds: support, PC building, application. Claiming, priority, /ticket add (let a user or role into a ticket), rename, close requests and closing all run through Tickety.

Arcane
Levels and XP. Nothing to manage.
Eco bot
Economy fun in #eco: /balance, /eco, /game, blackjack, slots, /gamble, gifts. Not support related.
Disboard
Server bumps in the bump channel.

Rules and Judgement

The calls you will have to make, and who to ask.

Personal information
Never ask users for it. If you see someone else asking, report it to Management.
Payments
Not permitted. No paid work, commissions or price negotiations. See the Policies page.
Auto-mute bot
If a user is flagged repeatedly, screenshot it, send it in the support ticket and contact Management. Pinging support roles directly gives you a 1-minute timeout too.
Ratings
Users rate you out of 10 in #ratings (2 hour slowmode). Take a bad rating professionally, read the feedback, and talk to a Senior if it was unfair.
Escalation
Anything you cannot solve or decide yourself: Management.

Staff Policies

The rules every specialist agrees to.

Use of AI

While AI tools can be valuable resources for assisting with technical support, they should be used responsibly and in moderation.

Guidelines

  • AI may be used as a supplementary tool for research only.
  • Direct copying of AI-generated responses is prohibited.
  • Your own technical knowledge should always be the primary resource.

Remember that while AI can provide helpful suggestions, our goal is to offer human-to-human support with personal attention to each user's unique situation.

Payments and Tipping Policy

No payments

Payments are not permitted. No paid work, commissions, paid 1-on-1 services or price negotiations of any kind through this server.

We appreciate that users may wish to reward exceptional service. However, to maintain professionalism and fairness, strict guidelines apply to accepting gratuities.

Permitted

  • Accepting a tip only if explicitly offered by the user first.
  • Communication via the ticket channel.
  • Communication via DMs only with prior permission from Senior Staff+ (SR+).

Prohibited

  • Soliciting or asking for a tip.
  • Setting a price for support services.
  • Requesting a specific amount.
Strict enforcement

Any specialist found requesting tips, setting prices, or negotiating amounts will be issued TWO strikes on a first offense, which can lead to removal from the team. Our support services are free; tipping is entirely optional and user-initiated.

Personal Information Policy

Specialists must refrain from asking users for personal information including but not limited to:

Important notice

In order to preserve the user's privacy and uphold strict ethical standards, specialists must avoid collecting any personally identifiable information unless absolutely necessary and explicitly consented to.

Prohibited information requests

  • Full name or any identifying aliases
  • Physical address or location details
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Identification numbers (SSN, etc.)
  • Financial information

Specialists must prioritize user privacy and only request information that is strictly necessary for the technical support task at hand. Any requests for personal information must be justified by absolute necessity and require explicit user consent.

Promotions

Our promotion system is designed to recognize and reward staff members who demonstrate consistent excellence in their roles.

Junior Specialist → Specialist

  • After taking several tickets and remaining active, Senior Staff+ will schedule an "Observation".
  • Senior Staff will complete an observation form evaluating your performance.
  • Promotion decisions are based on this formal evaluation.
  • Demonstrate consistent activity and quality responses.
  • Complete an introduction in #specialist-intros including: your name/nickname, where you're from, your areas of technical expertise, and why you joined the support team.

Specialist → Senior Specialist

  • Consistently high user satisfaction ratings.
  • Demonstrated leadership within the team.
  • Active participation in community discussions.
  • Willingness to mentor junior staff members.
  • Consistent activity over an extended period.
Note

Promotions beyond Specialist are evaluated based on overall contribution to the community, not just quantitative metrics. Leadership qualities, technical expertise, and dedication to helping others are all considered.

Inactivity Policy

To maintain an active and responsive support team, we require all staff members to remain engaged with our community.

Activity requirements

  • If you do not participate in the server in a 14 day period without warning, you can be given a strike, demoted, or removed.
  • Participation in community discussions is encouraged.
  • Extended absences should be communicated to leadership.
Example scenario

A staff member hasn't responded to any tickets or participated in server discussions for 3 weeks without notice. After attempts to contact them go unanswered, they would be subject to removal from the staff team.

We understand that real life takes priority, so communication about availability is always appreciated and will be taken into consideration.

Three Strike System

To maintain high standards of professionalism and service quality, we employ a three-strike system for policy violations.

First strike

Cause: First-time violations (e.g., breaking rules, unprofessional behavior, misuse of AI, soliciting or requesting a tip)

Result: Formal warning.

Duration: 14 days.

Second strike

Cause: Repeating violations, soliciting or requesting a tip

Result: Temporary restrictions & leadership review.

Duration: 14 days.

Third strike

Cause: Continued violations.

Result: Removal from staff team.

Appeal: After 30 days.

Important

Severe violations may skip the strike system entirely and result in immediate removal at the discretion of leadership. All strikes expire after 14 days if no further violations occur.
