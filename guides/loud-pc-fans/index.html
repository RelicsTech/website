PC Fans Too Loud? How to Quiet Them

Loud fans usually mean heat, dust or a bad fan curve. Find which fan makes the noise before you change anything.

  1. Find the noisy part. Check the fan speeds in HWiNFO and listen closely. The graphics card, the CPU cooler, the case fans and the power supply can all be the source.
  2. Clean the dust. Shut the PC down and blow the dust out of the fans, filters and heatsinks. Hold the fan blades still while you do it.
  3. Check the temperatures. If the CPU or GPU is hot, the fans have to spin fast. Fix the cooling first, see our guide on high CPU temperatures.
  4. Set a smoother fan curve. In the BIOS or a tool like Fan Control, set a low speed at idle and raise it slowly with the temperature. Big jumps make the noise come and go.
  5. Check for rattles. Tighten loose screws and move cables away from the fan blades. Rubber fan mounts cut vibration.
  6. Replace a worn fan. Grinding or clicking means a worn bearing. Replace it with a fan of the same size, usually 120 or 140 mm.

Not sure which part is loud? Describe the sound and we will help you find it.
