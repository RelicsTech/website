Keyboard Recommendation Questionnaire

Looking for a new keyboard? Fill out the questionnaire below so we can help you find one that suits your preferences and needs.

Answer all questions in a single message using Shift + Enter for line breaks.

1. Budget & Availability

How much are you willing to spend? Include your local currency, country and any preferred retailers.

2. Form Factor & Layout

What keyboard size and layout do you prefer?

E.g. Full-size, TKL, 75%, 65%, 60%, ANSI or ISO.

3. Switch Preferences

What type of switches do you prefer?

E.g. Linear, tactile, clicky, magnetic or no preference.

4. Design & Aesthetics

Do you have a preferred colour scheme, theme or overall design? Is RGB lighting important to you?

5. Customisation & Assembly

Would you be comfortable building or modifying a keyboard yourself, or would you prefer a prebuilt option?

6. Intended Usage

What will you primarily use the keyboard for? Do you play competitive games?

E.g. competitive gaming, casual gaming, programming, typing or office work.

7. Connectivity

Do you prefer wired, wireless or both?

8. Previous Keyboard Experience

Which keyboards have you used before? What did you like or dislike about them?

9. Additional Requirements

Are there any specific features or requirements you would like us to consider?

E.g. Rapid Trigger, adjustable actuation, hot-swappable switches, sound profile, keycap material or dedicated media controls.

The more information you provide, the more accurate our recommendations will be.
