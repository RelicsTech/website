Mouse Recommendation Questionnaire

Looking for a new mouse? Please fill out the questionnaire below so we can recommend something that suits your needs.

Answer all questions in a single message using Shift + Enter for line breaks.

1. Budget & Availability

How much are you willing to spend? Include your local currency, country and any preferred retailers.

2. Grip Style

How do you usually hold your mouse? (Palm, Claw, Fingertip or a hybrid grip)

Refer to the pinned grip guide and mark the areas where your hand touches the mouse.

3. Hand Measurements

What are your hand's length and width? Follow the measurement guide attached below.

4. Previous Mouse Experience

Which mouse are you currently using or have used in the past? What did you like or dislike about its shape, size and overall feel?

5. Personal Preferences

Is there anything specific you're looking for in your next mouse?

E.g. weight, wired/wireless, shape, buttons, colour or intended use.

The more information you provide, the more accurate our recommendations will be.
