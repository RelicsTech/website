Controller Recommendation Questionnaire

Looking for a new controller? Fill out the questionnaire below so we can help you find one that suits your needs.

Answer all questions in a single message using Shift + Enter for line breaks.

1. Budget & Availability

How much are you willing to spend? Include your local currency, country and any preferred retailers.

2. Platform Compatibility

Which platforms will you use the controller on?

E.g. PC, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch or mobile.

3. Games & Usage

What types of games do you primarily play? Include a few game titles as examples.

4. Connectivity

Do you prefer a wired controller, a wireless controller or one that supports both?

5. Features & Functionality

Which features are important to you?

- Hair triggers / trigger stops

- Macro or turbo functionality

- Remappable buttons

- Adjustable stick tension

- Modular or replaceable components

- Swappable face buttons (Xbox/Switch layout)

- Gyroscopic controls

- Vibration / rumble

Use Controller Features for a brief explanation of each feature.

6. Hand Measurements

What are your hand's length and width? Follow the measurement guide attached below.

You may skip this question if you cannot measure your hands.

7. Previous Controller Experience

Which controllers have you used before? What did you like or dislike about their comfort, ergonomics and functionality?

8. Stick Layout

Which analogue stick configuration do you prefer?

- Asymmetric (Xbox style)

- Symmetric (PlayStation style)

- Custom / No preference

9. Shape & Ergonomics

Which controller shape feels most comfortable to you?

- Xbox

- Nintendo Switch Pro

- DualShock 1–4

- DualSense

- Other / No preference

10. Additional Requirements

Is there anything else you're looking for in your next controller?

E.g. Hall Effect or TMR sticks, polling rate, battery life, weight or build quality.

The more information you provide, the more accurate our recommendations will be.
