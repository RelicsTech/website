PC Won't Turn On? Quick Checks

Most "dead" PCs are a loose cable or a switch, not a broken part. Go in this order and stop when it boots.

  1. Check the power source. Plug the PC into a different wall socket without a power strip. Make sure the switch on the back of the power supply (PSU) is on (I, not O).
  2. Reseat the power cables. Push the 24-pin motherboard cable and the 8-pin CPU cable in firmly. Both must click. Also check the front-panel power button header.
  3. Strip it down. Unplug everything except the CPU, one RAM stick, power and the monitor. Boot it. Add parts back one at a time.
  4. Reseat the RAM. Remove the sticks and press them back in until both clips click. Try one stick per slot if it still fails.
  5. Check the display cable. Plug the monitor into the graphics card, not the motherboard, unless you have no graphics card.
  6. Reset the BIOS. Unplug the PC and take out the coin cell battery for one minute, or use the CLR_CMOS jumper. Put it back and try again.

If it still stays dead, the PSU or the motherboard is the likely cause. Tell us what lights or beeps you get.
