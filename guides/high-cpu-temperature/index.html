CPU Too Hot? How to Fix High Temps

High temperatures make a PC loud, slow and unstable. Measure first, then fix the most likely causes in this order.

  1. Measure first. Use HWiNFO and note the idle and load temperatures. Roughly 30 to 50 °C idle and 60 to 85 °C in games is normal. Around 95 °C the CPU slows itself down.
  2. Clean the dust. Shut the PC down and unplug it. Blow dust out of the fans and heatsinks with compressed air. Hold the fan blades still so they don't overspin.
  3. Check the cooler mount. The cooler must sit flat and tight on the CPU. Remove any plastic film from the cold plate. An AIO pump must be plugged into a pump header and run at full speed.
  4. Replace the thermal paste. If the paste is several years old or you remounted the cooler, clean it off and apply a pea-sized dot of new paste.
  5. Fix airflow and fan curve. Use front fans as intake and rear or top fans as exhaust. Set a fan curve in the BIOS so the fans speed up as the CPU gets warmer.
  6. Check BIOS power settings. Turn off overclocks and automatic overvoltage. A small undervolt or lower power limit can cut the temperature a lot.

Still hot? Post your cooler, case and the temperatures you see, and a specialist will help.
