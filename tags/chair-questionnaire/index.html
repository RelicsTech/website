Chair Recommendation Questionnaire

Looking for a new chair? Fill out the questionnaire below so we can help you find one that suits your comfort, posture and preferences.

Answer all questions in a single message using Shift + Enter for line breaks.

1. Height & Weight

What is your height and weight?

This helps us recommend a chair with suitable dimensions and weight capacity.

2. Budget & Availability

How much are you willing to spend? Include your local currency, country and any preferred retailers.

3. Intended Usage

What will you primarily use the chair for, and how many hours a day do you typically spend sitting?

E.g. gaming, office work or general everyday use.

4. Sitting Habits

How do you usually sit? Do you sit cross-legged, tuck your legs underneath you or frequently change positions?

5. Posture & Ergonomics

Are there any posture-related concerns, discomfort or physical limitations we should consider?

E.g. lower back pain, neck discomfort or specific support requirements.

6. Design & Aesthetics

Do you have a preferred chair style, colour or material?

E.g. ergonomic office chair, gaming chair, mesh, fabric or leather.

7. Adjustability & Features

Are there any features you specifically want?

E.g. adjustable lumbar support, headrest, adjustable armrests, seat depth or reclining mechanism.

8. Previous Chair Experience

What chair are you currently using? What do you like or dislike about it?

9. Additional Requirements

Is there anything else you would like us to consider? Are you open to refurbished or second-hand chairs?

The more information you provide, the more accurate our recommendations will be.
